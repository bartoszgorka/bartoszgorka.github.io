I am a **Platform / DevOps & FinOps Engineer** working across the infrastructure and software engineering boundary.
I build and operate shared cloud infrastructure, **Kubernetes** platforms, observability, and cost governance for 20+ SaaS brands at [saas.group](https://saas.group), with a focus on **automation, reliability, security, and cost efficiency**.

My background is **backend development** with a strong emphasis on Domain Driven Design, **Elixir/OTP** principles, and modular software design.
I draw a lot of inspiration from distributed systems, Systems Thinking, and **artificial intelligence**, and I like treating infrastructure as a product: well-tested, easy to change, and understandable for the teams who use it.

The goal I constantly pursue is to build modern and reliable platforms that let businesses move fast without burning money.
My motto is: *Be the change you wish to see in the world*.

In private I am an enthusiast of volleyball and photography.
